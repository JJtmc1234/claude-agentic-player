TEAM CONTEXT — Manager + Employees
==================================

Hierarchy:

    Human (JJ)
        ↕  (bidirectional, 1:1)
    Manager (single agent)
        ↕  (1:many — Manager talks to each Employee individually)
    Employee-1  Employee-2  Employee-3  Employee-4  (...expandable)

The Human ONLY talks to Manager under normal operation. Employees DO
NOT communicate with the Human. Manager is the sole escalation point
for human-input decisions.

The Human retains a break-glass ability to interrupt any Employee's
work directly (Ctrl+C, in-session interrupt, force-stop). This is
NOT normal; it's an override.
