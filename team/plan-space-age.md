# Space Age Run — Master Plan (JJ, 2026-07-08)

> **Core rule:** Nauvis is the airport. Vulcanus is the capital.
> Build Nauvis good enough to reach space and support Vulcanus.
> The real empire is the **Vulpire** (Vulcanus base).

## Main vibe
Fun / learning / **default settings / no cheating**. Not a perfect speedrun.
Learn Space Age properly, enjoy the process, build toward the future base plan.

## High-level strategy
- Nauvis = compact **starter / launchpad**, NOT the final empire.
- First major arc: **Nauvis starter → first spacepack platform → Vulcanus trip → early Vulpire**.
- Vulcanus eventually becomes the central base/empire (the Vulpire).
- Keep Nauvis useful for rockets, resupply, backup — but **do not overbuild it**.

## This run's style — gentle rocket rush + learning
- Push toward rocket / platform / Vulcanus sooner rather than later.
- Still build & understand the basic systems properly.
- Do NOT skip important learning just to rush.
- Do NOT over-polish Nauvis. Do NOT make Nauvis a megabase.
- Keep builds readable enough for humans AND agents to understand.

## Nauvis design style
- Starter spaghetti is allowed.
- Keep clear **zones**: smelting · power · science · mall · oil · rocket/platform prep.
- Avoid massive central belt knots.
- Preserve working systems.
- **Ask JJ before major redesigns. Do NOT mass-deconstruct unless JJ approves.**

## Early Nauvis roadmap
1. Stabilize iron, copper, coal, stone.
2. Keep **autopack** running.
3. Build **logipack**.
4. Add **militpack** for safety/defense.
5. Build a small useful **mall**: belts, inserters, power poles, assemblers, miners,
   furnaces, pipes, undergrounds/splitters, ammo/turrets/walls.
6. Set up **oil**.
7. Automate **chempack**.
8. Prepare rocket parts + first platform materials.
9. Build **first platform** (mainly for spacepack).
10. Build/prepare **second platform** as Vulcanus taxi/cargo.
11. Establish Vulcanus foothold.
12. Begin early Vulpire planning.

## Rocket / platform philosophy
- **First platform:** mostly spacepack + learning space logistics. Safe, simple, repairable.
- **Second platform:** Vulcanus travel/cargo.
- Do NOT overengineer the first platform into a flying cathedral.
- DO make sure it survives — exploding in space is educational but annoying.

## Vulcanus plan
- Get there early-ish, after Nauvis can support it.
- Start with survival, scouting, power, basic production.
- Do NOT export heavily at first — build Vulcanus up locally.
- Once stable, Vulcanus becomes the main industrial base (the Vulpire).

## Role philosophy
Early miner/courier roles are useful at the start; **promote them later**.

**Early roles**
- Miner: ore, smelting, coal, stone.
- Courier: fuel, chest stocking, moving supplies.
- Builder: machines, belts, power poles, starter science.
- Scout: nearby resources, oil, biters, safe exploration.

**Later dynamic roles**
- Architect: zones, layout standards, no-touch areas.
- Industry Lead: mall, smelting, foundries, production modules.
- Science Lead: pack progression + bottlenecks.
- Platform Engineer: spacepack platform, cargo platform, repairs, ammo.
- Logistics Lead: rockets, cargo, resupply, planet-to-planet flow.
- Safety/QA Lead: power, deadlocks, defenses, shortages, broken builds.

## Manager behavior
- Friendly roadmap style. Give task tickets, but don't make it feel like corporate homework.
- Keep reports short.
- Approve small improvements independently.
- Ask JJ before major layout changes, risky expeditions, destructive redesigns.
- Prioritize learning + fun over perfect optimization.

## Report format (use every status report)
1. Current phase
2. What got done
3. Current bottleneck
4. Current risk
5. What JJ should do manually
6. Next recommended step
